Camino Ninja

Camino Ninja is a free app for walking the Camino de Santiago, made by Jakobsweg-Zentrale. It has offline maps, stage planning with distances and elevation, and hostel reviews from other pilgrims. It has no ads.

  • A pilgrim ninja poofs in at Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port.
  • It runs the Camino Francés, flipping over the Pyrenees. The km counter drops through Pamplona, Burgos, León, the Cruz de Ferro, rainy O Cebreiro and the 100 km stone past Sarria.
  • The route behind it gets marked with the yellow arrow's colour. At the cathedral a scallop-shell sunburst opens and the logo writes itself in.

About 15 seconds. Distances are rounded. Sources: caminoninja.com, App Store.
